Four ways it differs from a speed quiz

  • Confidence BiddingWager part of your points while you can only see the category and difficulty.
  • Safety NetPlayers in the bottom 30% earn a one-time Double Down or Shield.
  • Side-betsAfter you answer, see how the class is voting and bet on whether most got it right.
  • Group modeRandom teams vote on every wager and answer, and one captain breaks ties.
